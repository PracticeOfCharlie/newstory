**쫀디기 / 쫀드기**
- 남일제과 makes 쫀디기; its 부장 박성렬 says rules have been tightening for years and they now do thorough hygiene tests. https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/2013/04/27/20130427014002
- 네모 스낵 is a thin long rectangle of 쫀드기, 100-200 won ("단돈 100~200원"), in sweet-bulgogi and spicy flavours; you tear it along the grain and roll it into your mouth; grilling over fire makes it chewy and soft; it is still sold at elementary-school 문방구 (article dated 2020-01-13). https://www.insight.co.kr/news/263150
- Many remember swapping a 100-won coin for it. Same URL.
- 쫀드기 was made of wheat flour and corn starch, and had little taste of its own, some kids ate it for the sugar dusted on the surface; lightly grilled it gets soft and "야들야들", and kids took it home to grill it with friends (article 2018-05-18). https://www.insight.co.kr/news/121053
- Korean Wikipedia: popular 1960s-70s, sold at school-front 문방구; grillable on a portable grill or briquette fire; "World Cup flavour" pieces had different colours for different flavours. https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/쫀득이 (English version: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jjondeugi)
- Revival: air-fryer 180 degrees for 2-3 minutes; "불쫀드기" and "쫀드기 철판볶음" at 경주 황리단길; sold as bar snacks (article 2025-06-28). https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1060098

**호박꿀맛나 / 맛기차콘 (한진식품)**
- 한진식품 CEO 김영기 (42) makes 맛기차콘 and 호박 꿀맛나; "대기업보다 부족한 것은 포장과 마케팅뿐". https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/2013/04/27/20130427014002
- 호박꿀맛나 = wheat flour, corn powder and pumpkin powder shell around a syrup "honey" filling; kids asked the 문방구 아주머니 to toast it over a gas flame so the shell went soft; some kids squeezed out and ate only the honey and threw the unbaked shell away. https://www.insight.co.kr/news/121053 (2018-05-18)

**아폴로**
- 1969 by 김상규 at 우림제과 (founded 1965); name from the US Apollo lunar programme; company renamed 아폴로제과 in 1971; sales boom in the late 1990s (1997 crisis); by 2008 sales were slowing and costs up; the owner expected to close within two years; the snack "ceased to be produced by 2010, company closed by January 2013"; another Korean company began distributing it in 2011, made by the Chinese company 광동백성식품 to cut cost; packaging changed (original logo a girl, now a boy; old bags had one flavour, new bags mix flavours). English Wikipedia (which cites 위클리조선 2008-03-21 and 헤럴드경제 2018-09-21). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apollo_(candy)
- Korean Wikipedia, same lines, plus: in 2008 the price stayed at 100 won despite higher raw-material cost. https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/아폴로_(사탕)
- Ritual: most kids squeezed it with the front teeth; a few sucked the straw clean; it passed the 보건환경연구원 test as a "legal" food, though it was called the symbol of 불량식품. https://www.insight.co.kr/news/121053
- The Hankyung 2023 price article is NOT read (403 from the site). Do not quote 430 or 800 won.

**맥주사탕 / 페인트사탕 / 테이프 사탕 / 손바닥 사탕**
- 맥주 사탕: official name 타리토 캔디; contains synthetic pineapple and passion-fruit flavouring; was 100 won each in the past, about 500 now; kids pretended to be drunk; the foam part could cut your tongue. https://www.insight.co.kr/news/470133 (2024-05-31)

- 테이프 사탕: opaque starch candy rolled up like tape that you could bite off; if you got careless it unrolled "like toilet paper without a core", and you stuffed it all in your mouth. Same URL.
- Kids asked "맥주 먹을 거야? 아니면 콜라 먹을 거야? 손바닥도 먹고 싶긴 한데!", so a "콜라 사탕" existed next to 맥주/손바닥 사탕 (article calls the shop "매점이나 문구점"). https://www.insight.co.kr/news/470133

**차카니, 나나콘, 꾀돌이, 밭두렁**

### 2.1 쫀디기 (쫀드기, 쫀득이, 쫀듸기)

- **What:** chewy flat strip, mainly wheat flour and corn starch. [1S, English Wikipedia summary]. English Wikipedia says it was popular around the 1960s-70s, sold in stationery stores near schools as a cheap student snack, sweet, salty or both. [1S]. That means an old snack, see cohort flag.
- **Maker:** no single maker (many small firms). **[READ]** 남일제과 makes "쫀디기": its 부장 박성렬 (42) is quoted in the Seoul Shinmun 2013-04-27 article: stricter regulation "for several years", they now inspect hygiene thoroughly, "no big difference in process from big-company products", but anxiety remains. https://m.seoul.co.kr/news/2013/04/27/20130427014002 Brand: "네모 스낵" is a representative product. [1S, insight.co.kr/news/263150]. An "울산쫀드기" with a red core ("연필심" pencil-lead type) is a variant sold online (Gmarket listing). [1S, current retail].
- **Look (for prop):** thin flat rectangular strip, brown (sweet bulgogi-like) or reddish (spicy) coating, sold in a small clear or printed bag. The pencil-lead style is a thin round stick with a red centre. [1S for the pencil style, Gmarket; flat strip look from "thin long rectangle" in the earlier pack, 1S]. Packaging colours: NOT found, so keep generic.
- **Price:** 네모 스낵 was "100-200 won" in kids' memories, many remember trading a 100-won coin for it. [1S, insight.co.kr/news/263150]. Today about 490 won each on 11st. [1S, current retail]. Price specifically in 2008-2014: NOT found.
- **Wikipedia (ko, [READ], https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/쫀득이):** ko page says 쫀득이 was popular from the 1960s to the 1970s; main ingredients wheat flour and corn starch, a fried food; chewy texture; sweet, salty or both; commonly sold at 문방구 near schools as a cheap student snack; popular again as nostalgia; can be baked over a portable grill, briquette fire or air fryer. ## 4. The bigger story for a closer

### 4.1 불량식품 and the 4대악 (2013)

- **2013 policy:** the new government described 불량식품 as one of the "4대 사회악" and promised a strong crackdown. [2S: an MFDS 2013 webzine summary and a second MFDS document summary]. The MFDS (식품의약품안전처) was created on 2013 March by upgrading the 식품의약품안전청 (MFDS webzine, mfds.go.kr/webzine/201303/pdf/34.pdf) [1S].
- **The four evils: [READ].** 성폭력, 학교폭력, 가정폭력, 불량식품. Seoul Shinmun 2013-04-27 states it with Park as president (see above). Korean Wikipedia "4대 사회악" (https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/4대_사회악) [READ]: announced as a campaign pledge when Park was a candidate (cites SBS 2012-12-08), police set up units after she took office (뉴스와이 2013-02-27), and she instructed measures for eradicating the "4대 사회악" (YTN 2013-03-14). The ko page lists the four as 성폭력, 학교 폭력, 가정폭력, 불량식품 유통. Exact first-announcement date is still not read; a safe phrasing is "2013, the new government named 불량식품 one of the 4대악".
- **April 2013:** MFDS launched a government-wide 불량식품근절추진단 with the PM's office, the agriculture ministry, prosecutors and police. [1S, summary of an MFDS source].
- **MFDS 2013 work plan (21 March 2013):** fine of up to ten times the sales for 불량식품 sellers and permanent expulsion of intentional offenders. [1S, 식약청 업무보고 summary].
