Outline stage only. The full script comes after the user picks an option.

**Lineup (settled by the user):** 쫀디기, 아폴로, 맥주사탕, 차카니. Other snacks appear only as a passing mention or a background prop: 페인트사탕 as the "rival" jar next to 맥주사탕, and the 한진식품 maker quote with no product shown.

## Tags

- **[V]** = in the research pack's "VERIFIED FACTS SAFE FOR THE SCRIPT" box (read at source). These facts are firm. The source is given briefly: `263150`, `121053`, `246238` and `470133` are insight.co.kr article numbers, `seoul 2013` is the Seoul Shinmun 2013-04-27 article, `wikitree 2017` is wikitree 294024, and `ko-wiki` / `en-wiki` are Wikipedia.
- **[R-]** = marked [READ] in the pack's older section 4 but left out of the verified box. The box says "anything not here is not yet safe", so the fact-checker must confirm these.
- **[1S] / [UNC] / [MEM]** = unverified, as defined in the pack.

## Notes for the fact-checker and lead

1. **The 쫀디기 shop toasting does not match the verified box.** The coordinator's summary says the shop 아주머니 toasted 쫀디기 over gas. In the verified box, that detail belongs to **호박꿀맛나** (`121053`). For 쫀디기, the box only supports these:
   - grilling over fire makes it chewy (`263150`);
   - kids took it home to grill with friends (`121053`);
   - it can be grilled on a portable grill or briquette fire (ko-wiki).

   So every outline shows 츄리 toasting it herself on a small portable grill. No line says the shop did it.
2. **The 맥주사탕 price sources conflict.** `121053` says "100원에 두 개" (맥주/페인트 사탕). `470133` says "100 won each in the past". Neither gives a year. The outlines use "백 원에 두 개", and this needs a ruling.
3. **차카니 has no price in any source.** No line states one.
4. The pack's tag legend still says "[READ] ... None exist in this pack". That line is out of date.
5. 4대악 lines say "나라에서" and never name or show the president, even though the source names her.

### Build

- **Sets:** shopfront and counter.
- **Props:**
  - 100-won coins (generic face);
  - 쫀디기 strip with tear and roll animation and sugar sparkle;
  - straw bundle with a powder puff;

### Risks

- **Unverified facts it depends on:**
  - the 맥주사탕 "두 개에 백 원" price, which conflicts with "백 원 each" (note 2);
  - the 차카니 price: the coin count on screen must not imply one, so in beat 7 she pays with an unspecified slide of coins, not one counted coin (note 3);
  - the 그 뒤로 애들이 없어졌대 line, which is [V] but is the Wikitree writer's own claim. Keep the "~래" hearsay ending.
- **Premise:** none of the prices is dated to 2008-2014 except 아폴로. The other prices are "back then" memories from articles written in 2018-2024. The budget frame needs the fact-checker to accept "그때" prices with no year.
- **Tone:** 맥주사탕 gets one wobble and an explicit "사탕이야". No drink props, no slurring.
- **Cohort:** 쫀디기 and 아폴로 both peaked before this cohort (1960s-70s and the late 1990s). The outline never says "only ours".

## Recommendation

**I would now make C (먹는 법).** With the user's lineup fixed and the rituals verified, C fits the format best: 츄리 is physically doing the thing in every shot, and the specific rituals are exactly the "헐, 이거 완전 까먹고 있었다" material:

- tearing along the grain;
- 앞니로 밀기 vs sucking the straw;
- 맥주파 vs 페인트파;
- licking the 차카니 powder off each finger.

It is also the cheapest to build: one set, and the "N교시" label template can be reused. Its weakness is a thinner explanation chain, so beats 4 and 8 are non-negotiable.

**Safest: B (학교 앞 200미터).** Its spine is entirely verified, it has the strongest "what happened" story, and the school gate set pays off later. The cost is that the Green Food Zone and the melamine sweep give "헐, 몰랐다" more than "까먹고 있었다". If the user wants the strong closer, a good hybrid is C's beats 1-7 followed by B's beats 6 and 8 (4대악, -37%), ending back at the counter.

**A** is fun but the most exposed: its premise needs a price for every coin, the 맥주사탕 price sources conflict, and 차카니 has no price at all.
