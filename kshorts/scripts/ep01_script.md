# EP01 츄리의 문방구 과자 먹는 법: shooting script (rev. 2, after fact-check)

Option C as chosen, with no 4대악 or 불량식품 angle. It is an affectionate "this is how we ate them" lesson in four 교시. The turns come from inside the rituals:
- the wrong bite vs the right tear;
- 앞니파 vs 쪽쪽파, names 츄리 openly makes up;
- 맥주파 vs 페인트파, which the source says friends split into.

The ending callback: she has eaten everything during the lesson, so she splits the last 쫀디기 along the grain and gives the viewer half, with homework.

- **Length:** 42 lines and 133 어절. That is about 62 s of speech at a brisk pace, and about 72-75 s with the SFX gags, the home-grill insert and label holds.
- **Narration for the voice model:** `/home/user/kshorts/scripts/ep01_voice.txt`, the same lines one sentence per line with numbers in Hangul. Line numbers below match the voice file.
- **Shots:** a new shot at least every line (lines run about 1-3 s), and every line has her doing something with her hands or the props.
- **Tags:** [V] = from the research pack's VERIFIED FACTS box, re-checked at source in `ep01_factcheck.md`. The letters A-E refer to that report's page list. Full URLs are in "Facts used" at the end.
- **Era wording:** no year, "우리 때" or "~잖아" claims of shared experience. Secondhand memories take hearsay endings (~래, ~대) or plain past tense (~있었어).

## Sets

1. **문방구 counter (main set, reusable).** A low wooden counter with a glass-front display, a shelf of round plastic candy jars with lids behind it, and the viewer's side of the counter where the POV hand rests. **There is no grill on this set.**
2. **문방구 shopfront (insert, reusable).** A sliding door with a wire snack rack beside it, used for one pass at the start of 4교시.
3. **츄리네 식탁 (home insert, new, reusable for later home episodes).** A small kitchen table by a window and her own little portable one-burner grill. It is used for lines 9-10 only, with a mosaic transition in and out, so the grilling plainly happens at home.

## Props (all generic look-alikes, no real logos or brand marks)

- **쫀디기:** thin, long, flat rectangular strips about the length of a hand, with a faint lengthwise grain. Two colour variants (a design choice): brown for sweet and reddish for spicy. White sugar specks on the surface, also a design choice. Sold in a small clear unprinted bag. It is rigged to tear lengthwise into thin strings, to roll up, and to soften and droop when toasted.
- **아폴로:** a bundle of short, thin plastic straws holding pale powder, in assorted colours (a design choice), in a clear unprinted bag. It needs a small powder-puff particle effect.
- **맥주사탕:** a small hard candy shaped like a stubby bottle with a distinct foam part on top, kept in a candy jar. Brown with a white cap is a design choice; the label area is blank.
- **페인트사탕 (background only):** a candy jar of blue candies next to the 맥주사탕 jar. Simple blue shapes, never handled up close.
- **차카니:** short yellow cylindrical pieces dusted with red powder, in a plain unprinted bag. Red-powder residue on her fingertips is a texture swap.
- **Small portable grill (home insert only):** a toy-sized one-burner tabletop grill with a wire net and a soft flame glow, with a "치익" SFX.
- **POV hand:** the viewer's hand in a plain dark sleeve, used in lines 1-2 and 40-41.
- **Pointer:** a wooden ruler she uses as a teacher's pointer.
- **2D overlays:**
  - the yellow key-term label ("N교시" template);
  - a split card "앞니파 / 쪽쪽파" with a small tag "(츄리 작명)";
  - a split card "맥주파 / 페인트파";
  - small bubble particles for 맥주사탕;
  - a "숙제" sticky-note card reading "180도 예열 → 2~3분" (screen text only, so digits are fine).
