# EP01 츄리의 문방구 과자 먹는 법: shooting script

Option C as chosen, with no 4대악 or 불량식품 angle. It is an affectionate "this is how we ate them" lesson in four 교시. The turns come from inside the rituals:

- the wrong bite vs the right tear;
- 앞니파 vs 쪽쪽파;
- 맥주파 vs 페인트파.

The ending callback: she has eaten everything during the lesson, so she splits the last 쫀디기 along the grain and gives the viewer half, with homework.

- **Length:** 43 lines and 128 어절. That is about 58 s of speech at a brisk pace, and about 70 s with the SFX gags and label holds (the cough, the toast, the finger licks, the realisation).
- **Narration for the voice model:** `/home/user/kshorts/scripts/ep01_voice.txt`. It is the same lines, one sentence per line, with numbers in Hangul. Line numbers below match the voice file.
- **Shots:** a new shot at least every line (lines run about 1-3 s), and every line has her doing something with her hands or the props.
- **Tags:** [V] = from the research pack's VERIFIED FACTS box. The URLs are in "Facts used" at the end.

## Sets

1. **문방구 counter (main set, reusable).** A low wooden counter with a glass-front display. Behind it is a shelf of round plastic candy jars with lids, and on the counter a small tabletop wire-grill with a blue gas-flame glow. The viewer's side of the counter is where the POV hand rests.
2. **문방구 shopfront (insert only, reusable).** A sliding door and a wire snack rack beside it, used for one pass in the 4교시 transition. Mosaic transition in and out.

## Props (all generic look-alikes, no real logos or brand marks)

- **쫀디기:** thin, long, flat rectangular strips, about the length of a hand, with a faint lengthwise grain texture. There are two colour variants, brown (sweet) and reddish (spicy), with fine white sugar specks on the surface, sold in a small clear bag with no print. It is rigged to tear lengthwise into thin strings, to roll up, and to soften and droop when toasted.
- **아폴로:** a bundle of short, thin plastic straws in assorted pastel colours, filled with pale powder, in a clear bag with no print. It needs a powder-puff particle effect.
- **맥주사탕:** a small brown hard candy shaped like a stubby bottle with a white "foam" cap, sold loose from a candy jar. The label area is blank.
- **페인트사탕 (background only):** a candy jar of bright blue candies on the shelf next to the 맥주사탕 jar. Simple blue shapes; it is never handled up close, and no shape claim is made.
- **차카니:** short yellow cylindrical pieces dusted with red powder, in a plain bag with no print. Red-powder residue on her fingertips is a texture swap.
- **Small tabletop grill:** a one-burner wire grill with a soft blue-and-orange flame glow and a "치익" SFX.
- **POV hand:** the viewer's hand, plain dark sleeve, used in lines 1-2 and 41-42.
- **Pointer:** a wooden ruler she uses as a teacher's pointer.
- **2D overlays:**
  - the yellow key-term label ("N교시" template);
  - a split-screen card "앞니파 / 쪽쪽파";
  - a split card "맥주파 / 페인트파";
  - a small "거품 주의" sign;
  - a "숙제" sticky-note card with "180도 2~3분" (this is screen text, so digits are fine).

**Notes for the fact-checker:**
- **Line 9 (grill):** the line only claims you should toast it. 츄리 toasts it herself on her own small grill. The script does not say the shop toasted 쫀디기 (that detail belongs to 호박꿀맛나 in the box).
- **Line 12:** the source says "some kids" (일부). The line keeps that: "먹는 애들도 있었어".
- **Line 18:** the labels "앞니파 / 쪽쪽파" are 츄리's own coinage for the two verified ways of eating 아폴로. They are not presented as historical names. "맥주파 / 페인트파" are from the source.
- **Line 22:** the source says friends (친구들) split into the two factions, and the line says the same.
- **Line 42:** the air-fryer method is a 2025 revival detail, presented as homework, not as how it was eaten back then.
- **Lines 6 and 25:** "가로로 물면 하수야" and "난 맥주파" are her opinions, not claims.
- **Prices:** none are stated anywhere, including for 맥주사탕 and 차카니.
- **Left out on purpose:** 불량식품, 4대악, the crackdown, melamine, the Green Food Zone and the China-made 아폴로.
