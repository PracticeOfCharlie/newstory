## Rules check

- **Prices:** none are spoken or shown for 맥주사탕 or 차카니. The only number on screen is "180도 2~3분". Pass.
- **불량식품, 4대악, crackdown:** none appear in the voice file or the shot table. The word appears in the script only in the "left out on purpose" note. Pass. Two lines lean toward it:
  - Line 24 ("숨겨야 됐지") carries a hint of "forbidden snack", because its source reason is parents scolding about 불량식품. It is mild kid-sneaking humour and can stay. If the lead wants zero hint, use the softer wording below.
  - Lines 30 and 31 (foam cuts the tongue) are a small injury warning. That is borderline against "no safety-scare material". It is sourced and playful, but it is a bodily-harm warning. Soften it, or cut line 31.
- **Real people:** none. Pass.
- **맥주사탕 pretend-drunk moment:** Pass. Line 29 "사탕이야, 사탕" settles it as a candy joke, the clink is with candies, and there is no beer prop. Keep the sway short and the label at "취한 척". The 딸꾹 hiccup bubble is optional, and the character is a uniformed student, so cutting it is the safer choice.
- **Ritual attributed to the wrong snack:** none found. Tearing and grilling go with 쫀디기, front-tooth squeezing and straw sucking with 아폴로, pretend-drunk and the foam with 맥주사탕, and finger sucking with 차카니. The shop-owner toasting belongs to 호박꿀맛나 and is correctly not used.
- **Present-day detail as past practice:** only the air fryer (line 42), and it is framed as homework. Pass.
- **Era (2007-2015):** nothing in the narration states a year, but sources B and D carry no cohort evidence, and B explicitly describes the late 1990s. Only source C names "90~00년대생". The show's premise is 2007-2015. Keep any on-screen year or "우리 때" wording out of these lines, and keep the past-tense "있었어" form rather than "잖아".
